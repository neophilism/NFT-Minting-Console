## 5. Collector-Only Allocation From the Next Ten NFT Releases

If the Creator offers additional batches or collections of NFTs for sale after the Androids Of The Vine series, ten percent (10%) of the NFTs in each of the first ten such releases (each a “Qualifying Release”) will be reserved exclusively for members of the Galinsky Collectors Circle and will not be offered for sale to the general public. The reserved quantity will be rounded down to the nearest whole NFT.

The collector-reserved NFTs in each Qualifying Release will be available to eligible Galinsky Collectors Circle members on the price and material economic terms established for that Qualifying Release, subject to availability. No individual member is guaranteed a particular NFT or allocation merely by Circle membership.

Each collector-reserved NFT will remain available exclusively to eligible Galinsky Collectors Circle members for one (1) year beginning on the date that NFT is first offered to eligible collectors. During that one-year period, the NFT will not be offered for sale to the general public.

If a collector-reserved NFT has not been purchased by an eligible collector by the end of that one-year period, the Creator may retain it indefinitely or donate it without charge to a museum. Any such museum donation must be conditioned on the museum's written agreement that the donated NFT may be held and displayed by the museum but may never be sold, assigned, pledged, or otherwise transferred to any person or entity, except that the museum may return the NFT without charge to the Creator. The museum donation agreement may establish minimum public-display or public-access conditions and may provide that, if those conditions are not satisfied, ownership automatically reverts to the Creator or the Creator may require return of the NFT without charge. After any such return, the Creator may retain the NFT or donate it to another museum subject to the same or different museum-specific display or public-access conditions, but the NFT remains permanently ineligible for sale to the general public.

Any museum donation or display arrangement under this Section is intended solely to facilitate public access to, preservation of, or exhibition of the donated Work. No Owner or Galinsky Collectors Circle member has any right to require a museum donation, museum placement, exhibition, or display. The Creator undertakes no obligation to select museums, arrange exhibitions, secure museum placement, maintain museum relationships, or undertake any museum-related activity for the purpose of promoting, supporting, or increasing the market value of any NFT or generating a financial return for any Owner or collector. Any museum-related activity undertaken by the Creator is undertaken independently for artistic, preservation, exhibition, or public-access purposes and not as a service performed for NFT holders.

This Section does not obligate the Creator to create, complete, or offer any future NFT release. It applies only if and when the Creator independently elects to offer a Qualifying Release. Once ten Qualifying Releases have occurred, no further reservation obligation exists under this Section.

## 9. Creator Royalty on Secondary Sales

Secondary sales of this NFT are subject to a creator royalty of **six percent (6%)** of the gross secondary-sale price, payable to the royalty recipient identified by the Androids Of The Vine collection contract. The collection contract permanently reports that rate and recipient through ERC-2981. Compatible marketplace sales may additionally be subject to ERC-721C-compatible transfer validation intended to enforce payment of the royalty.

Ordinary direct wallet-to-wallet transfers may remain permitted by the collection's transfer policy. If a direct wallet-to-wallet transfer is in substance a compensated secondary sale that does not use an enforcing marketplace, the transfer mechanism cannot determine the sale price, and the parties remain responsible for honoring the six percent (6%) creator royalty obligation.
